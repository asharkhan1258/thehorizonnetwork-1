Privacy Policy

Horizon Network LLC ("Horizon Network LLC," "we," "us," or "our") provides website design and development services and operates the website located at www.thehorizonnetwork.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information when you visit our Site or engage us for our services (collectively, the "Service").

We do not sell, rent, or trade your Personal Information to third parties. By using our Site or Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you contact us, request a quote, or hire us for a project, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, business name, mailing address, project requirements, and any files, content, or images you provide for your website. If you make a payment, our payment processor collects your billing information directly; we do not store your full card details on our servers.

We also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit our Site, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect to prepare quotes, deliver your website design and development project, communicate with you about your project's progress, process one-time payments, respond to inquiries, and improve our Site and services. Horizon Network LLC charges a one-time fee for each service we provide; we do not offer subscriptions or recurring billing of any kind, and we will never charge you without your prior authorization for that specific project.

COOKIES AND TRACKING

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep the Site functioning properly, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use our Site. We also use analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to help us measure traffic and improve the Site's performance. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, though doing so may affect certain features of the Site.

SHARING YOUR INFORMATION

We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business, such as payment processors, hosting providers, and analytics services. These providers only access the information necessary to perform their specific function and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose. We may also disclose your information if required to do so by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Horizon Network LLC or others.

DATA SECURITY

We take reasonable, industry-standard measures — including SSL encryption — to protect your Personal Information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

DATA RETENTION

We retain your Personal Information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including any project files or records needed for future support, invoicing, or legal compliance. You may request deletion of your information at any time, subject to any legal or contractual obligations that require us to retain it.

YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your Personal Information at any time by contacting us at the details below. You may also opt out of marketing communications by using the unsubscribe link in any email we send or by contacting us directly.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our Service is intended for individuals and businesses aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with Personal Information, please contact us so we can remove it.

THIRD-PARTY LINKS

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites, including portfolio and client references. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page, and material changes will be communicated to you via email or a prominent notice on our Site. Your continued use of our Site or Service after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
